Mandatory data: Battery chemistry; critical raw materials; materials used in the cathode, anode, and electrolyte; hazardous substances; impact of substances on the environment and on human health or safety.

Overview

Properties

battery materials

Information on the detailed composition of the battery.

Information on the detailed composition must include materials used in the cathode, anode and electrolyte.

Materials in the battery should be reported above a concentration of 1% weight by weight.

DIN DKE Spec 99100:2025-02 chapter reference: 6.5.3-6.5.4

hazardous substances

Information on hazardous substances other than mercury, cadmium, or lead contained in the battery.

Hazardous substances also being substances of very high concern, as defined under the RegulationEC No1907/2006 (“REACH Regulation”) should be reported on if exceeding a threshold of 0,1% weight by weight.

is critical raw material

The battery passport must contain information on the critical raw materials present in the battery.

The information on the critical raw materials must also be provided on the battery label. Per Annex VI, Part A(10), critical raw materials must be reported if present in the battery in a concentration of more than 0,1 % weight by weight.
